The **2026 Hong Kong Forum on Neutron Scattering: Energy Materials and
Applications** will be held from **7 to 11 December 2026** at Eminence Galaxy,
Room 11-231, LAU, City University of Hong Kong.

The Forum is sponsored by the **Center for Neutron Scattering at CityUHK**, and
brings together researchers from academia and industry, together with
specialists from major international neutron scattering facilities, to discuss
the latest developments in neutron scattering and synchrotron techniques and
their applications to energy materials — batteries, fuel cells, hydrogen
storage, energy conversion materials and nuclear energy.

Participants share recent findings and exchange views on the current challenges
in the field, and students and early-career scientists in Hong Kong and the
surrounding area have the opportunity to learn the fundamentals and
applications of neutron scattering.

**Registration is free of charge.**

Programme overview — a five-day meeting with talks in the mornings and
afternoons, group discussions, and a welcome dinner on the first evening:

| Day | Date | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | 7 Dec (Mon) | Check-in; welcome dinner |
| Day 1 | 8 Dec (Tue) | Opening and welcome remarks; Talks 1–10; group photo |
| Day 2 | 9 Dec (Wed) | Talks 11–18 |
| Day 3 | 10 Dec (Thu) | Continuation of technical sessions |
| Day 4 | 11 Dec (Fri) | Closing session |

Meals are provided on campus, with evening dinners for invited participants.
Wi-Fi is available via "Wi-Fi.HK via CityU" and through institutional
accounts on "eduroam".

For the full agenda and registration, please watch this space or contact the
Center for Neutron Scattering directly.
