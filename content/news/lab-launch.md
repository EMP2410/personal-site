The **JC STEM Lab of Energy and Materials Physics**, led by Professor Yang Ren,
was officially launched at the inaugural ceremony for the first group of JC STEM
Labs supported by **The Hong Kong Jockey Club Charities Trust**, on **3 July
2025**, with **HKD 9.35 million** in funding.

The ceremony brought together senior university leaders and partner
organisations to celebrate the Lab's establishment and to highlight its
mission: advancing interdisciplinary research in energy and materials physics,
and nurturing local STEM talent.

The successful start-up marks a key milestone towards strengthening Hong
Kong's research capacity in energy and materials physics, and towards
sustained collaboration between academia, industry and the broader scientific
community.
