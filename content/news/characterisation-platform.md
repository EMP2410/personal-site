The JC STEM Lab of Energy and Materials Physics has completed the
establishment of a comprehensive **materials preparation and physics
characterization platform**, with the capacity to take a material from
synthesis to structure to working device.

Headline items on the platform:

- **Hong Kong's first benchtop X-ray Absorption Fine Structure (XAFS)
  spectrometer**, providing local element-specific electronic and local
  structural analysis without waiting for a beamline proposal.
- **Rigaku SmartLab** surface X-ray diffractometer.
- **Laser-based ARPES–MBE system** for surface and electronic structure studies.
- **Battery constant-temperature testing cabinets**, inert-atmosphere
  **gloveboxes**, and a **conditional mixer** for electrode and composite
  preparation.
- **High-temperature stages** for in-situ and temperature-dependent
  characterization.

The platform was designed around a simple requirement: that a local student
can bring a sample to the instrument, get a result in an afternoon, and use
it to decide what to do next. Measurement that requires a journey is
measurement that only a few people will ever do.
