Members of the group have been running a full programme of in-situ and
operando experiments across three international facilities.

**China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS).** In-situ neutron experiments on
energy materials, undertaken as part of our collaboration programme on
neutron-based battery materials research.

**SPRING-8, Japan.** Synchrotron soft X-ray absorption spectroscopy, giving
element-specific information on the unoccupied electronic states of our
materials.

**SOLEIL, France.** Two complementary campaigns: in-situ X-ray absorption
spectroscopy, following a material through a reaction as it happens, and
ultra-fast X-ray absorption spectroscopy, resolving the first picoseconds
after a photoexcited charge transfer.

These campaigns are where our students learn the instruments properly. Being
responsible for your own sample and your own scan — with the beamline
scientist a floor away rather than in the next building — is the training
that cannot be reproduced at home.
