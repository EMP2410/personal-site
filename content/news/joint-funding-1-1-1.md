Building on the foundation of the metallic glasses collaboration, CityUHK,
CityUHK (Dongguan) and the industry partner **Dongguan Yihao Metal
Technology** jointly applied for the Guangdong–Hong Kong Universities "1+1+1"
Joint Funding Scheme, and the project has been successfully funded with a total
of **RMB 2 million and HKD 1 million**.

The award demonstrates how the Lab uses synchrotron and neutron large-scale
facilities as a platform for building domestic collaboration in both education
and research. It highlights the effectiveness of cross-border collaboration
among CityUHK, CityUHK (Dongguan) and industrial partners in promoting joint
research, resource sharing, talent development and technology translation in
the Greater Bay Area.
