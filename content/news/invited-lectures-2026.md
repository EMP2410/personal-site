Recent invitations and exchanges in mainland China:

- **Visit and invited seminar at the Suzhou Laboratory** — 8 July 2026.
- **Featured presentation at Huaqiao University** — 26 June 2026.
- **Technical exchange with Xiamen Tungsten Co. (XTC) New Energy Materials
  Research Institute** — 27 June 2026.
- **Invited talk at ATL headquarters in Ningde** — 4 August 2025.

These engagements run in both directions. They bring our current work to
groups working on the same problems with different instruments, and they bring
students into contact with the industrial questions that motivate the science
— which is the point of a lab that sits between a beamline and a factory
floor.
