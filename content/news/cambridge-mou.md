Prof. Ren has signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the **Department of
Materials Science and Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge**, formalising
the CityUHK joint laboratory.

The collaboration was initiated to establish global academic and research
links between the Department of Physics at CityUHK and the Department of
Materials Science and Metallurgy at Cambridge. The MoU provides a formal basis
for extended overseas research training for local students, opening routes for
joint supervision, shared beamtime and co-supervised projects.

The practical value is in the pipeline: students who have trained at CSNS,
ISIS, SOLEIL or SPRING-8 return to CityU able to drive an experiment, and they
carry that experience into the joint laboratory with Cambridge — and back out
into the local programme again.
